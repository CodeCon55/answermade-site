Questions

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural, but it doesn't pretend to be a person. If a caller asks, it tells them honestly that it's an automated assistant for your business.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. Calls you don't answer forward to your receptionist, and we walk you through turning that on with your phone provider.

What if it gets something wrong?

You test it before it goes live, and we fix anything you're not happy with. If your prices or hours change, tell us and we update it.

What happens to my customers' information?

It's used only to help you follow up with your customers. It's never sold, and it's only stored with the trusted services that run your receptionist. You or your customers can ask us to delete it at any time. See our privacy notice.

Can I cancel?

Yes. There's no long contract. Just email us before your next 30-day period renews, and you won't be charged again.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any business that gets phone calls it can't always answer: auto repair shops, plumbing and HVAC, salons, gyms, barbershops and more.
